Machu Picchu
Peru

the Incan citadel terraced into a ridge high in the Andes, wrapped in cloud forest with the peak of Huayna Picchu standing guard behind it. Want to hike the trail up and catch it as the morning fog burns off the ruins.

Monaco
Monaco

a lakeside town built right into the hillside — terracotta rooftops, a bell tower, cypress trees leaning over the water, mountains rising behind it. The kind of place worth slowing down in for a few days.

Bioluminescent Lake
Puerto Rico

a bay where microorganisms in the water glow electric blue every time it's disturbed — kayaking through it at night under the stars, leaving a trail of light behind the paddle.

Volcán de Fuego
Guatemala

an active volcano, glowing lava streaking down its slope against a night sky full of stars, city lights scattered in the valley far below. Want to camp on the neighboring ridge and watch it erupt after dark.

Petra
Jordan

the Treasury carved directly into sandstone cliffs, framed by the narrow canyon you walk through to reach it. An ancient city hidden in the rock — want to see the facade catch the light as it opens up in front of you.

Pyramids of Giza
Egypt

the pyramids silhouetted at dusk, sunlight catching just the upper faces of the stone against a darkening sky. One of the oldest things you can still stand in front of.
